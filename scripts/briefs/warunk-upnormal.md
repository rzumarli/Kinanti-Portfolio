## Slide 6: Desk Research

Targets the youth market segment aged 15-25 years (beritasatu, 2023).

During the Covid-19 pandemic, Warunk Upnormal's sales decreased by up to 40% (bisnis.com, 2023).

Many outlets were closed.

To adapt to new post-covid consumer habits, Upnormal implementing Pay at Table feature in the Upnormal App.

Providing promos for purchasing delivery via GrabFood & GoFood.

Some outlets renamed as Upnormal Coffee & Roaster, but with the same menu list.

Social media platforms: Instagram, Twitter, Facebook and Tiktok.

Will add self service Indomie cooking.

Warunk Upnormal on Google News, May 2023

## Slide 8: Interview Summary

3 out of 4 participants never been to Upnormal again after trying it once. The reasons are:

- 2 participants said that the foods are very ordinary, nothing special
- The foods are overpriced
- The foods are just Indomie and it's quite expensive.

Participants might go to Upnormal again, if:

- 2 participants said that if there's an event by their favorite artist
- There's a promo
- There's new menu

## Slide 9: Interview Summary

What participants like about Upnormal:

- 3 participants said that the place is nice
- There's a playzone (UNO, UNO Stacko)

Participants usually look information about restaurant from:

- 3 participants look from Instagram
- Google
- Google Maps

3 participants stated that they rarely open website of a restaurant. 1 participants stated that they often open website of a restaurant.

Information that the participants look for are:

- All participants look for menu & price list
- 2 participants said they look for reservation contact/channel
- Outlet gallery
- Location

## Slide 10: Interview Summary

What participants think about why Upnormal are quite and many outlets were closed:

- 2 participants said that Upnormal is outdated, they don't keep up with trends
- Only hype at the beginning, there's nothing interesting anymore
- Menus are overpriced, inferior to other restaurant with lower price range

What participants think about what might make Upnormal crowded again:

- 2 participants stated if the foods are upgraded to better taste
- If they change the name, because Upnormal is outdated
- If they add more variety of games
- If they add more variety of menu, especially the sharing menu

## Slide 33: Conclusion

Most of the participants don't want to go back to Upnormal after trying it once. To make them keep coming to Upnormal again, I made this solution based on what will attract them: something special about Upnormal and a worth it service.

The "Reward for Feedback" will keep customer coming to Upnormal and spend money to redeem the reward. The new menu and event will make customer always attracted to something new. The uniqueness of self-service will make customer keep coming back to Upnormal.

## Main sections / themes of the deck

- **Background and problem framing**: the cafe's decline (About, Objective, Context), many outlets closed and falling market interest.
- **Research**: desk research (youth segment 15-25, sales down up to 40% during Covid), in-depth interviews with 4 participants, interview summaries, research summary, competitor analysis (benchmarked mainly against Mie Gacoan), user persona, How Might We, and idea prioritization.
- **Core finding**: customers do not return because the food tastes ordinary, the menu is overpriced for what it is, and there is nothing special about Upnormal anymore.
- **Solution track 1, Reward for Feedback**: a survey accessed with a Receipt ID, in three parts (scalable, options, free text), with a voucher reward sent by email.
- **Solution track 2, General Suggestions**: seasonal menus and artist events (live music, meet & greet) with a minimum-purchase entry condition.
- **Solution track 3, Self-Service Indomie components**: special sauce recipe, various free and paid toppings, complete Indomie variants including rare and premium ones, an automatic ramen machine, and tips & tricks cooking videos on TV.
- **Closing**: conclusion, next steps (usability testing and iteration), reflection, key learnings, and references.
