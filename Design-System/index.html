Ornaments

Hand-drawn scribbles are the signature decoration. They appear as loose glyphs inside the hero headline, as hanging ribbons on the mobile Experience cards, and as quiet watermarks inside cards.

Squiggle · Blue#2185E3 · pairs with the Bubblegum card
Squiggle · Orange#F85C36 · pairs with the Sky card
Squiggle · Pink#FE7FC4 · pairs with the Peach card
Rabbit glyphReplaces the “o” in “people-driven” in the hero headline
Pairing ruleRibbon and ornament colors never match their own card. Bubblegum gets blue, Sky gets orange, Peach gets pink.
StrokeRound linecap, 3.34 units on the squiggles, 3.76 on the rabbit. Always a single continuous stroke.
WatermarksInside Experience cards the scribble returns as a large white or black watermark, cropped by the card edge.
